Preserve the actual distinctive Klipper upper symbol geometry: two broad diagonal arms converge as a V toward the lower center; the central filament descends vertically and turns left at the bottom. Keep the internal filament separation visible using white negative space, so the original multicolor geometry remains recognizable in pure black and white. Do not substitute the letter K, a generic printer, an aperture, or a new logo.
Output: exactly ONE perfectly square 1:1 image, ideally 1024x1024 pixels. Pure white opaque background. A perfectly straight black square border inset evenly 64 pixels from all four canvas edges, approximately 16 pixels thick, sharp 90 degree corners. Center a bold black Klipper extruder mark within the border, occupying roughly 68% of the canvas width, with comfortable white breathing room. Use flat black shapes and precise smooth edges, like industrial hazard signage in the provided Portal reference.
Constraints: monochrome pure black and pure white; no blue tint, texture, perspective, gray shading, gradients, shadows, text, letters, watermark, extra symbols, caption, or panel grid. The border and canvas are truly square. Only one icon.
